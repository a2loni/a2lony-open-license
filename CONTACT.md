# Contact and voluntary agreements

Each rights holder may voluntarily publish a contact method for questions, proposals, or requests for additional permission. Contact is not required to exercise permissions already granted by the license, and a rights holder is not required to maintain a channel, respond, or accept a proposal.

General questions may be raised through [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/a2loni/a2lony-open-license/issues) once the public repository is available. Do not post confidential information or personal data in public issues.

Parties may enter separate agreements about rights they actually control. A useful record identifies the parties, material, applicable version, scope, duration, conditions, date, and verifiable evidence of acceptance. Private agreements do not remove rights held by third parties or reduce rights already granted to unrelated recipients.

# Changelog

## 1.0-RC2 — 2026-11-08

English-first release candidate. `LICENSE.txt` is now a complete English legal text and the controlling text for RC2. The 15-section structure, broad copyright permissions, patent grant, attribution and trademark rules, third-party rights, voluntary direct agreements, warranty disclaimer, liability limits, and cure process are preserved from RC1.

This is a translation and clarification candidate, not an independent legal validation. The section-by-section equivalence report documents editorial choices and questions for professional review.

## 1.0-RC1 — 2026-11-07

First public Spanish-language release candidate with permissions for commercial use, patent grants, attribution, trademark clarity, voluntary direct agreements, warranty disclaimer, limitation of liability, and a cure process for remediable breaches. The historical tag `v1.0-RC1` remains unchanged.

Both candidates are release candidates. The project is not OSI-approved, has no official SPDX identifier, has not received independent legal review, and may change before a stable 1.0 release.

This release is published for public consultation and evaluation. It is not a certification of legal validity, enforceability, or universal compatibility. Users and distributors must determine whether it is suitable for their material and jurisdiction.

RC1 was initially drafted in Spanish. RC2 provides a complete English candidate and identifies that English text as controlling for this release. Any future OSI submission would need to meet OSI's then-current requirements for language, documentation, and review. This candidate may change before a stable 1.0 release.

Potential review topics include translation equivalence, jurisdictional interpretation, patent scope, termination and cure mechanics, consumer-law limits, competition-law considerations, and interoperability with established license identifiers. These topics are documented for review and do not silently alter `LICENSE.txt`.

# RC2 Equivalence Report

## Scope and status

This document compares the English `LICENSE.txt` in A2lony Open License 1.0-RC2 with the Spanish `LICENSE.txt` preserved in the immutable `v1.0-RC1` tag.

The comparison is a drafting and translation review, not legal advice, certification, or independent counsel review. It cannot establish that the two texts have identical legal effect in every jurisdiction.

For RC2, the English `LICENSE.txt` is expressly identified as the controlling legal text. The Spanish RC1 is historical and informative for comparison only.

## Drafting choices and known ambiguities

1. “Licensor” and “Recipient” were selected as standard English legal terms for “Otorgante” and “Destinatario.”
2. “Royalty-free, no-charge” is retained to reflect both the absence of royalties and the absence of a required payment.
3. “Necessary infringement” in the patent grant is intentionally narrow and is not a promise that every use is non-infringing.
4. “Closed-distribution applications” is used for applications whose distribution model does not expose source code; this wording should be reviewed for clarity before a stable release.
5. “Cure” and “curable breach” are used for the 30-calendar-day subsanación mechanism. The legal effect of cure and termination is jurisdiction-dependent.
6. RC2 expressly makes English controlling. This is a clarification for this candidate, not a claim that the translation has been legally certified.

## Deliberate non-changes

- The 15-section architecture was retained.
- Commercial use, modification, redistribution, larger works, patent terms, attribution, trademarks, contributions, voluntary agreements, warranty exclusions, liability limits, third-party rights, and cure mechanics were not intentionally removed.
- No text was copied in full from Apache, MIT, Blue Oak, or another third-party license.
- No automatic fines, criminal penalties, absolute legal immunities, or mandatory source-disclosure requirement were added.

## Required review before a stable 1.0

Obtain independent legal review of the English RC2 text, compare it against the Spanish RC1 tag, review patent and termination clauses in the intended jurisdictions, and verify compatibility with any third-party components or projects that may adopt the license. Until that work is completed, this remains a public release candidate only.

# How to use A2lony Open License 1.0 RC2

1. Confirm that you control or are authorized to license the material.
2. Identify the covered material and version clearly in the project documentation.
3. Include the complete `LICENSE.txt` with source and executable redistributions.
4. Preserve relevant copyright, license, attribution, patent, and third-party notices.
5. Identify material changes in a reasonably accessible way.
6. Keep third-party licenses separate and unchanged.
7. Do not imply sponsorship or trademark permission that was not granted.
8. Use the optional agreement template only for separate permissions over rights actually controlled by the parties.

This guide explains the candidate text; it does not add obligations or replace the license. Read the English [LICENSE.txt](../LICENSE.txt) as the controlling legal text for RC2. The Spanish RC1 is historical and remains available through its Git tag.
